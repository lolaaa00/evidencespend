CORE PRIMITIVE — DO NOT REDESIGN
EVIDENCESPEND is semantic double-spend protection for evidence.

It must keep the following separation:
1. Deterministic policy decides which previous evidence is relevant to compare.
2. Exact relevant content_digest equality is deterministically SAME_EVIDENCE.
3. GenLayer consensus is used only for relation classification between distinct frozen evidence descriptors:
   - SAME_EVIDENCE
   - DERIVED_EVIDENCE
   - OVERLAPPING_EVIDENCE
   - INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE
   - AMBIGUOUS
4. Deterministic code alone enforces reuse modes:
   - SHAREABLE
   - EXCLUSIVE
   - INDEPENDENT_REQUIRED
5. Ambiguous or blocked decisions must never increase accepted_count or accepted-spend history.
6. The relation classifier must remain policy-blind. Do not expose reuse mode, remaining counts, authorization or desired outcome to the LLM.
7. Do not silently turn this into a truth oracle. EVIDENCESPEND does not prove evidence truth, authority, issuer identity or URL authenticity.
